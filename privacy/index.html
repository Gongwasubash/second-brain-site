Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Second Brain AI respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we access via TikTok and how we use it.

1. Data we access

  • Basic profile: open_id, display name, avatar (scope: user.info.basic).
  • Your video list: video IDs, titles, creation times (scope: video.list).
  • We do not access your password, private messages, or other users' data.

2. How we use it

Data is used only to show you your own content library and help you plan educational posts. We do not sell data or share it with third parties except hosting providers (Netlify) needed to run the site.

3. Storage

TikTok access tokens are stored only in your own browser session where possible and never published. You can revoke access anytime via TikTok Settings → Security → Manage apps.

4. Contact & deletion

To request deletion of your data, email supremegongwanepal@gmail.com and we will delete it within 7 days.
